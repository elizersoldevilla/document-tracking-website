CLEAR — Document Request Guidelines
===================================

1. Before you request
   - Confirm the document you need (transcript, certification, clearance, letter).
   - Pick the professor who will review and endorse your request. Requests are
     routed only to the professor you select.

2. Submitting a request
   - Go to New request, choose your professor, and enter a title (3-120
     characters) and a description (10-2000 characters) explaining what you need
     and why.
   - Submitting locks the request into the review queue. You will see it on your
     dashboard immediately with the status SUBMITTED.

3. Following the workflow
   - SUBMITTED          Sent to your selected professor.
   - UNDER_REVIEW       The professor is reviewing your request.
   - SIGNED_APPROVED    The professor has approved and signed it.
   - ENDORSED_TO_ADMIN  Forwarded to the administration office.
   - ADMIN_PROCESSING   The administration is processing the document.
   - READY_FOR_PICKUP   The document is ready at the designated office.
   - COMPLETED          The request has been claimed and closed.

4. Decisions and remarks
   - Every decision is recorded with the reviewer's name, time stamp, and any
     remarks. Open the Decision Hub to see them, or the Revision Desk to focus
     on requests with remarks.

5. Picking up the document
   - When the status is READY FOR PICKUP, present your request reference
     (REQ-XXXXX) at the designated office. After claiming it, the admin office
     marks the request COMPLETED.
