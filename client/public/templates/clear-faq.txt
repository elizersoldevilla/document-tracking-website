CLEAR — Frequently Asked Questions
===================================

Q: How do I submit a document request?
A: Open New request, pick the professor who will review it, enter a title and a
   description of at least 10 characters, then submit. The request enters the
   queue immediately.

Q: How do I track a request?
A: The dashboard shows your current status, next action, and workflow steps.
   The Application page shows submission dates and requirements; the document
   page shows the full status history.

Q: Can I edit a request after submitting it?
A: Requests are immutable after submission. Their status evolves through
   recorded decisions instead. Submit a new request for a different document.

Q: Who can approve my request?
A: Your selected professor reviews and approves, then endorses it to the admin
   office. The administration processes endorsed requests and releases them.

Q: What do remarks mean?
A: Remarks are notes recorded by a professor or admin alongside a decision.
   They appear on your Revision Desk and in your notifications.

Q: Why did I get a "too many attempts" error on the login page?
A: The login is rate limited to 10 attempts per 15 minutes per IP address.
   Wait for the window to reset and try again.

Q: Where is my data stored?
A: Requests, statuses, and history are stored on the CLEAR server database.
   Notification read flags are stored locally in your browser.
